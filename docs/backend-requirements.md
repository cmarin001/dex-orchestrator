## Collection and item semantics

Positions are generated by the worker, and exits are commands. Trade fills, history, equity publications and audit events are append-only projections. Candidates come from Scanner. Registry strategies, providers and chains already have canonical IDs and are configured through validated commands. Generic create, update and delete routes for those read models would bypass their lifecycle. The numbered matrix identifies the currently needed commands and detail views. Watchlist, settings sync and rejection reversal require their own consumers and state policies before implementation.

## Verification after approval

Each numbered remote action needs a test of the HTTP method and body, initial receipt, worker result, idempotent replay, and missing-endpoint behavior. Each numbered data read needs a projection shape and browser rendering test, including empty and unavailable values. A local action needs a no-network regression check. REST and MCP tool outputs must be compared at the service boundary. The approval gate covers the matrix and the unresolved decisions above.
